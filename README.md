# JeepQuest (The Jeep Game)

A road-trip vehicle-spotting game from **Sparkasia Studios**, sister title
to [PlateQuest](https://github.com/JcWolF76/PlateQuest). Spot Jeeps and
other vehicles on the road, tally your points, and see who racks up the
highest score on the trip.

**Play:** https://jcwolf76.github.io/JeepQuest-TheJeepGame-/

## How it works

Every vehicle category is worth a fixed number of points per spot. Jeeps
are split into two classes:

- **OG** — original/classic body style (Wrangler, Sahara, CJ, etc.)
- **NOG** — everything else (Compass, Gladiator, Cherokee, Grand
  Cherokee, Wagoneer, Grand Wagoneer, etc.)

Both Jeep classes are worth 1 point per spot. Other vehicles score
higher the rarer they are, up to the 50-point Tuktuk.

## Status

Solo mode is live: profiles, trips, three game modes (Solo, Family,
Challenge), and local share codes for trading progress. Multiplayer
(shared live game codes, à la PlateQuest) is planned as a follow-up.
